Privacy Policy — Video Gen Management

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Video Gen Management is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel More Than Your Bank to publish the channel's own videos, after the owner has reviewed and approved each one, and to follow how they perform. It is not offered to anyone else.

It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data handled by Google is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, through Google's OAuth, it requests these scopes, all on the owner's own channel:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the owner's approved videos, as private videos scheduled to publish at a time the owner chooses;
  • youtube.force-ssl: to add each video's subtitles, custom thumbnail and playlist entry, and to post one comment on a Short linking to the full video;
  • youtube.readonly: the channel's ID and its list of videos;
  • yt-analytics.readonly: the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, audience retention and similar reports).

It never deletes videos, and it does not read comments, messages or any other person's data.

How the data is used and stored

The access token and the channel's own statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how they perform. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Video Gen Management's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token then stops working, and the locally stored statistics are deleted within 30 days, or at once on request.
