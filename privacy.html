Privacy Policy — Video Gen Management

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Video Gen Management is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel More Than Your Bank to follow how the channel's own videos perform. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, through Google's OAuth, it requests two read-only scopes:

  • youtube.readonly: the channel's ID and its list of videos;
  • yt-analytics.readonly: the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, audience retention and similar reports).

It cannot upload, edit or delete anything, and it does not read comments, messages or any other person's data.

How the data is used and stored

The statistics and the access token are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are used only to show the owner how the channel's videos perform. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Video Gen Management's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token then stops working, and the locally stored statistics can be deleted by the owner.
