Terms of Service — Video Gen Management

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Video Gen Management is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel More Than Your Bank. It publishes that channel's own videos, after the owner has reviewed and approved each one, and reads that channel's own statistics. It has no other users and no public sign-up; these terms describe how it may be used.

YouTube Terms of Service

Video Gen Management uses YouTube API Services. Anyone who uses it agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who may use it

Only the channel owner, signed in with Google OAuth on the owner's own YouTube channel. It may be used only to manage content the owner has the rights to publish.

What it does

  • uploads videos the owner has approved, as private videos scheduled to publish at a time the owner chooses;
  • adds each video's subtitles, custom thumbnail and playlist entry, and posts one comment on a Short linking to the full video;
  • reads the channel's own statistics to show the owner how its videos perform.

It never deletes videos. Nothing is published without the owner's approval.
